**DRAFT — marketplace terms for designers. Not final.**

These terms apply when you sell invitation templates, themes or calligraphy work on Daawa's marketplace, in addition to our general Terms of use.

## 1. Your designs

- You confirm that every design you upload is your own work, or that you hold the rights to sell it, including fonts, images and ornaments.
- You keep the copyright. You give Daawa a licence to show, sell and render your designs for hosts, and to use them in marketing for the marketplace.
- Each host who buys a design may use it for **one event**. Invitations already sent keep working even if you later edit or remove the design.

## 2. Review

- We review every new design and every change before it goes on sale. We may reject a design that breaks these terms, our quality rules or the law, and we tell you why.
- We may take a design off sale, or suspend a designer account, if a design infringes someone's rights or hosts are misled.

## 3. Prices and your share

- You set your prices per currency (SAR, SYP, USD) within the limits shown in the portal.
- You receive **70%** of the net price of each sale, unless we agree another share with you in writing. Net means after any discount code and without VAT.
- Designs you include in the Signature plan are used by Signature hosts without a separate purchase; they are paid from a monthly pool under rules we will publish before it starts.

## 4. Refunds

- If a host is refunded under our refund policy, your share of that sale is reversed.

## 5. Payouts

- Statements are issued on the 1st of each month for the month before, per currency, once your balance reaches the minimum shown in the portal; smaller amounts carry over.
- We pay by bank transfer to the details you give us. You are responsible for your own taxes.

## 6. Changes

- We may update these terms. We will tell you before a change that affects your share or payouts takes effect.
