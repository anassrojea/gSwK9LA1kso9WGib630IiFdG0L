Cookies and local storage are small files your browser keeps. We use as few as we can.

## 1. Necessary (always on)

- **Session and form protection:** to keep you signed in, keep your invitation draft before you create an account, and protect forms against forged requests. They go when you sign out or the session ends.
- **Language:** to remember Arabic or English.
- **Your consent choice:** we keep in your browser (local storage) whether you accepted measurement tools, so we don't ask every time.

## 2. Measurement (only with your consent)

If you accept, we use PostHog to measure how the site and the host dashboard are used: pages visited, invitation creation steps and completed payments. We never sell this data or use it for advertising. For this we keep a random identifier in your browser.

If you decline, this tool is never loaded.

## 3. Guests' invitation pages

Invitation pages (links starting with /i/) set no cookies and load no measurement tool. We only count opens for the host, and count clicks on the "Design yours" link anonymously, with no identifier.

## 4. Changing your mind

You can change your choice at any time:

:consent_button

or by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. Read more in the [Privacy policy](:privacy_url).
